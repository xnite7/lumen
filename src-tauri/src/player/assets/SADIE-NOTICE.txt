sadie-d1-brir.wav: 13 binaural room impulse responses (KU100, 48 kHz, 0.27 s)
taken from the SADIE II Database, subject D1 BRIR set, unmodified apart from
selection and trimming.
sadie-d1-dome.wav: the same set's 29 responses at or above ear level (0.17 s,
faded out at the end), for placing Atmos sounds around and above you.
